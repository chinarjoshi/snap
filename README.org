Whatever. Here's a shitty overengineered AI-generated ANTLR grammar to parse natural workout descriptions into org-mode tables.

** Example

Input:
#+begin_example
ate a banana beforehand
squat 8 8 8 225
bench 3x8 155
#+end_example

Output:
#+begin_example
ate a banana beforehand

| Exercise| 1     | 2     | 3     |
|----------|-------|-------|-------|
| Squat   | 8@225 | 8@225 | 8@225 |
| Bench   | 8@155 | 8@155 | 8@155 |
#+end_example

** Syntax

| Notation         | Meaning                        |
|------------------+--------------------------------|
| =squat 225=      | 1 set of 8 reps @ 225          |
| =squat 8 8 225=  | 2 sets of 8 reps @ 225         |
| =squat 3x8 225=  | 3 sets of 8 reps @ 225         |
| =squat 3x8x225=  | 3 sets of 8 reps @ 225         |
| =squat 8 by 225= | 3 sets of 8 reps @ 225         |
| =squat 3 by 8 by 225= | 3 sets of 8 reps @ 225    |

Prose lines (no numbers) appear above the table. Inline notes (words after numbers) go in a Notes column.

** Go CLI
